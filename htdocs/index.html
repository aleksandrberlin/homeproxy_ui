VPNs

Add your VPN provider links, then "Refresh All" to download servers. Expand to see servers and connect rule sets.

Routing mode is not set to "custom". Rule routing only works in custom mode.

Custom Servers

Manually add VPN servers (WireGuard, etc.) and connect rule sets to them.

Custom Rules

Add domains or IP ranges and choose how to route them: VPN, Direct, or Block.

Rule Sets

External rule set files. Choose outbound per list: VPN, Direct, or Block. Add links to .srs files.

Devices

Route all traffic from a static DHCP device through the VPN, Direct, or Block. Choose "Default" to apply no special route. Domain rules take precedence — a device's route applies to the rest of its traffic.

Settings

Global settings for HomeProxy subscriptions and routing.

Connections

Live connections through sing-box. Shows destination, routing outbound, and traffic.
